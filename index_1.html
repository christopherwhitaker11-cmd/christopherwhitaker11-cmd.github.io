Missed-call text-back demo line · Albany, Georgia

Christopher Whitaker builds automation for local service businesses. The demo line, (229) 744-7347, shows business owners how a missed call can be followed up automatically by text.

How the demo line works

When someone calls (229) 744-7347 and the call is not answered, they hear a short voice message letting them know a text is coming. They then receive one text asking what they need. Further texts are sent only if they reply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
