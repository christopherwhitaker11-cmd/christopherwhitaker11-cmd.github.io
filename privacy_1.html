Privacy Policy

Christopher Whitaker · Effective November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Christopher Whitaker ("we," "us") collects and uses information through the missed-call text-back demo line, (229) 744-7347, and related text messaging ("SMS") services.

Information we collect

  • Your phone number, when you call or text (229) 744-7347.
  • The content of text messages you send us, such as your name, what you need, your address or town, and the best time to reach you, if you choose to share them.
  • Basic call and message records, such as the date and time of a call or message.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your missed call by text and continue the conversation if you respond.
  • To pass the details you share along so someone can call you back.
  • To honor opt-out (STOP) and help (HELP) requests.
  • To maintain and improve the service and keep records required by law.

We use service providers to operate the service, including a telecommunications provider to send and receive calls and texts, and an automation and AI provider to process messages and draft replies. They may use your information only to provide these services to us.

SMS opt-in data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the service providers described above that help us deliver the messages.

Opting out

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You will receive one confirmation message and no further texts. Reply HELP for help.

Data retention and security

We keep conversation records only as long as needed for the purposes above, and we use reasonable measures to protect them. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us at the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
